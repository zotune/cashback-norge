# Privacy Policy — Cashback Norge

**Last updated:** 1 May 2026

## What data does the extension collect?

Cashback Norge does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

## How does it work?

When you visit a website, the extension checks the site's hostname (e.g. `example.com`) against a locally cached list of cashback offers. This check happens entirely on your device.

## Network requests

The extension periodically downloads an updated list of cashback offers from GitHub Pages (`zotune.github.io`). No personal information, browsing history, or cookies are included in this request.

## Local storage

The extension stores:
- A cached copy of the cashback offer list (to reduce network requests)
- UI preferences (e.g. whether the notification panel is collapsed)

This data never leaves your device.

## Third parties

The extension does not share any data with third parties.

## Contact

If you have questions, open an issue at https://github.com/zotune/cashback-norge/issues
